
#Webcrawling

- It is used to index websites .
- Tools like spiders are used to crawl webs 
- Used for search engine indexing , seo audits ( look for broken ,links and outdated audits ) , content agrigation . 


#Webscrapping

- It is a automated way to extract specific information from the website .
- Some example uses of web-scrapping : price monitoring , brand management , building ai models .  



Sometimes they are both used together , as a scrapper is good when you have to run it on a single page but to navigate around the website and multiple pages , it needs to be paired up with a crawler .
